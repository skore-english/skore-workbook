The Assessment Dilemma in Mass Higher Education

고등교육의 대중화와 평가 방식의 구조적 모순 분석

STEP 01 원문정독 STEP 02 디코딩 STEP 03 논리구조 STEP 04 어법드릴 STEP 05 실전변형
STEP 01 · FULL PASSAGE & CORE CLAIM

전체 지문 정독 및 핵심 명제

Higher education has grown from an elite to a mass system across the world. In Europe and the USA, increased rates of participation occurred in the decades after the Second World War. Between 2000 and 2014, rates of participation in higher education almost doubled from 19% to 34% across the world among the members of the population in the school-leaving age category (typically 18-23). The dramatic expansion of higher education has been marked by a wider range of institutions of higher learning and a more diverse demographic of students. Changes from an elite system to a mass higher education system are associated with political needs to build a specialised workforce for the economy. In theory, the expansion of higher education to develop a highly skilled workforce should diminish the role of examinations in the selection and control of students, initiating approaches to assessment which enable lifelong learning: assessment for learning and a focus on feedback for development. In reality, socio-political changes to expand higher education have set up a ‘field of contradictions’ for assessment in higher education. Mass higher education requires efficient approaches to assessment, such as examinations and multiple-choice quizzes, with minimalist, impersonal, or standardised feedback, often causing students to focus more on grades than feedback. In contrast, the relatively small numbers of students in elite systems in the past allowed for closer relationships between students and their teachers, with formative feedback shaping the minds, academic skills, and even the characters of students.
[핵심 요지] 고등교육이 엘리트 중심에서 대중적 시스템으로 확장되면서 이론적으로는 발전적 피드백과 평생학습을 지향했으나, 실제로는 대규모 인원 처리의 효율성 요구로 인해 표준화된 시험과 최소한의 피드백이라는 모순적 평가 체제(Field of Contradictions)를 낳았다.

직독직해 및 어휘
[직독직해] 엘리트 체제에서 대중 고등교육 체제로의 변화는 경제를 위한 전문화된 인력을 양성하려는 정치적 요구와 연관되어 있다.
[핵심 어휘] be associated with (~와 관련되다), specialised workforce (전문화된 노동력/인력)

[핵심 어휘] in theory (이론상으로는), diminish (줄이다, 감소시키다), initiate (시작하다, 착수하다), lifelong learning (평생 학습)

[핵심 어휘] in reality (실제로는), socio-political (사회정치적인), field of contradictions (모순의 장/영역)

직독직해 및 어휘

[핵심 어휘] multiple-choice quiz (선다형 퀴즈), minimalist (최소한의), impersonal (인정미 없는, 비인격적인), standardised (표준화된)
9. In contrast, the relatively small numbers of students in elite systems in the past allowed for closer relationships between students and their teachers, with formative feedback shaping the minds, academic skills, and even the characters of students.
직독직해 및 어휘
[직독직해] 대조적으로, 과거 엘리트 체제의 상대적으로 적은 수의 학생들은 학생과 교수자 간의 더 긴밀한 관계를 가능하게 했으며, 형성 평가적 피드백이 학생들의 사고, 학업 기술, 심지어 인격까지 형성해 주었다.
[핵심 어휘] allow for (~을 가능하게 하다), formative feedback (형성적 피드백 / 발전을 돕는 피드백), shape (형성하다)
STEP 03 · LOGICAL ARCHITECTURE

지문 맞춤형 논리 구조도 (현상적 팽창 ➔ 이론적 이상 ➔ 현실의 모순 및 대조)

[배경·현상] Expansion of Higher Ed

고등교육의 양적 팽창

전후 전 세계적 진학률 급증(19%➔34%) ➔ 경제 발전을 위한 전문화된 인력 양성 요구로 엘리트에서 대중 체제로 전환됨.

[이상 vs 현실] Theoretical Ideal vs Reality

이론적 기대와 모순의 발생

이론상으로는 시험 축소 및 발전적 피드백을 기대했으나, 실제로는 대규모 학생 수용으로 인해 평가의 모순(Field of Contradictions) 발생.

[체제 대조] Mass System vs Elite System

대중 체제와 엘리트 체제의 대조

대중 체제는 효율적 객관식 시험·최소 피드백으로 성적에만 집착하게 만들며, 과거 소수 엘리트 체제의 인격적·형성적 피드백과 극명히 대비됨.

[문항 1] 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은? (어휘 추론)

Changes from an elite system to a mass higher education system are associated with political needs to build a specialised workforce for the economy. In theory, the expansion of higher education to develop a highly skilled workforce should ① diminish the role of examinations in the selection and control of students, initiating approaches to assessment which enable lifelong learning: assessment for learning and a focus on feedback for development. In reality, socio-political changes to expand higher education have set up a ‘field of ② contradictions’ for assessment in higher education. Mass higher education requires ③ efficient approaches to assessment, such as examinations and multiple-choice quizzes, with ④ personalised feedback, often causing students to focus more on grades than feedback. In contrast, the relatively small numbers of students in elite systems in the past allowed for ⑤ closer relationships between students and their teachers, with formative feedback shaping the minds, academic skills, and even the characters of students.
① diminish
② contradictions
③ efficient
④ personalised
⑤ closer
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ④ (personalised ➔ impersonal 또는 standardised / minimalist)
해설: 대중 고등교육 체제에서는 대규모 인원을 효율적으로 평가해야 하므로 개인 맞춤형 피드백이 아니라 최소한이고 비인격적이며 표준화된 피드백(minimalist, impersonal, or standardised feedback)이 제공됩니다. 따라서 '개인화된'을 뜻하는 personalised는 문맥상 어긋납니다.

[문항 2] 다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오. (빈칸 추론)

Changes from an elite system to a mass higher education system are associated with political needs to build a specialised workforce for the economy. In theory, the expansion of higher education should diminish the role of examinations, initiating approaches to assessment focused on developmental feedback. In reality, however, socio-political changes to expand higher education have ________________________________________. Mass higher education demands quick and standardized testing methods with impersonal feedback, which paradoxically leads students to prioritize numerical grades over constructive educational feedback.
① abolished the competitive hierarchy among various academic institutions
② established an ideal environment for personalized formative evaluations
③ created a paradoxical situation regarding educational assessment
④ shifted administrative emphasis entirely from workforce supply to research
⑤ strengthened reciprocal interactions between students and instructors
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③
해설: 이론적으로는 발전적 피드백과 평생학습을 지향했으나 실제로는 효율적인 객관식 시험과 기계적 평가로 귀결되어 모순을 낳았다는 원문의 'set up a field of contradictions for assessment'를 '교육 평가에 관한 역설적/모순적 상황을 만들어냈다(created a paradoxical situation regarding educational assessment)'로 패러프레이징한 ③번이 가장 적절합니다.

[문항 3] 주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오. (순서 배열)

Higher education has grown from an elite to a mass system across the world. The dramatic expansion of higher education has been marked by a wider range of institutions of higher learning and a more diverse demographic of students.

(A) In contrast, the relatively small numbers of students in elite systems in the past allowed for closer relationships between students and their teachers, with formative feedback shaping the minds, academic skills, and even the characters of students.

(B) In reality, socio-political changes to expand higher education have set up a ‘field of contradictions’ for assessment in higher education. Mass higher education requires efficient approaches to assessment, with standardised feedback causing students to focus more on grades than feedback.

(C) Changes from an elite system to a mass higher education system are associated with political needs to build a specialised workforce. In theory, this expansion should diminish the role of examinations, initiating approaches focused on feedback for development.
① (A) - (C) - (B)
② (B) - (A) - (C)
③ (B) - (C) - (A)
④ (C) - (A) - (B)
⑤ (C) - (B) - (A)
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ⑤ ((C) - (B) - (A))
해설: 고등교육의 대중화라는 배경이 제시된 후, (C)에서 경제적 요구와 이에 따른 이론적 기대(In theory: 시험 축소 및 발전적 피드백)를 언급합니다. 이어 (B)에서 In reality를 통해 대중 교육 체제가 낳은 평가의 모순과 표준화된 시험 중심 현실을 비판하고, (A)에서 In contrast를 통해 과거 소수 엘리트 체제의 긴밀한 사제 관계 및 형성적 피드백의 가치를 대비하는 (C)-(B)-(A) 순서가 가장 자연스럽습니다.

[문항 4] 다음 요약문의 빈칸 (A), (B)에 들어갈 말을 [조건]에 맞게 작성하시오. (서술형)
