The Necessity of Intellectual Property Protection
for Knowledge-Based Markets

지식 기반 사회와 지식재산권(IP)의 독점적 보호 — 원문 정독부터 실전 킬러 변형 4문항까지 연결하는 5단계 완전 학습 워크북입니다.

STEP 01 · FULL PASSAGE & CORE CLAIM

전체 지문 정독 및 핵심 명제

With the rise of the networked communications environment and the continuing progress of scientific advances, knowledge has become an increasingly key factor in fostering innovation, growth and health care technology and in sustaining cultural creativity. In addition, the ability of people to acquire, access and control knowledge is strictly related to questions pertaining to the ownership of knowledge. At present, intellectual property rules are the principal tool for controlling the creation, diffusion and use of knowledge and related goods and services. In order to have a market in knowledge, intellectual property protection is considered a fundamental instrument for creators of knowledge securing and maintaining exclusive access rights to knowledge-based products. For example, in the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industry, where development of new products requires substantial investments with long incubation periods, it is generally acknowledged that companies need exclusive right in order to recover research and development costs.
[핵심 요지] 지식 기반 경제에서 창작자의 연구개발 비용 회수와 정상적인 지식 시장 형성을 위해서는 지식재산권 제도를 통한 독점적 접근 권한(Exclusive Access Rights) 보장이 필수적이다.
STEP 02 · CHUNKING & VOCABULARY

문장별 직독직해 및 핵심 어휘 디코딩

[핵심 어휘] networked communications environment (네트워크 통신 환경), foster (촉진하다, 육성하다), sustain (유지하다)

직독직해 및 어휘

[핵심 어휘] pertaining to (~에 관련된 / 자동사구), ownership (소유권), acquire (습득하다)
3. At present, intellectual property rules are the principal tool for controlling the creation, diffusion and use of knowledge and related goods and services.

[핵심 어휘] intellectual property rules (지식재산권 규범), diffusion (확산, 보급), principal (주요한)

직독직해 및 어휘

[핵심 어휘] substantial investments (상당한/막대한 투자), incubation period (숙성/개발 기간), recover (회수하다)
STEP 03 · LOGICAL ARCHITECTURE

지문 맞춤형 논리 구조도 (현상 ➔ 핵심 원리 ➔ 실증 사례)

[배경·현상] Context

지식 가치 급증과 소유권 대두

통신망과 과학 발전으로 지식이 핵심 자산화 ➔ 지식의 접근·통제 문제는 결국 소유권(Ownership)의 쟁점으로 직결됨.

[주장·원리] Core Principle

지식 시장의 전제 = 독점권 보장

지식 시장이 성립하려면 창작자에게 배타적 독점 권리(Exclusive Rights)를 주어야 하며, 지식재산권이 핵심 기제로 작동함.

[사례·근거] Case Study

제약·바이오의 R&D 회수 모델

개발 기간이 길고 막대한 자본이 소요되는 산업은 독점권 없이는 투자금(R&D Costs) 회수가 불가능함을 실증 근거로 제시.

STEP 05 · EXAM SIMULATION (4 PROBLEMS)

고등부 수능/내신 실전 변형 문제 (4문항 체제)

[문항 1] 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은? (어휘 추론)

With the rise of the networked communications environment and the continuing progress of scientific advances, knowledge has become an increasingly key factor in fostering innovation, growth and health care technology and in sustaining cultural creativity. In addition, the ability of people to acquire, access and control knowledge is strictly related to questions pertaining to the ① ownership of knowledge. At present, intellectual property rules are the principal tool for controlling the creation, ② diffusion and use of knowledge and related goods and services. In order to have a market in knowledge, intellectual property protection is considered a fundamental instrument for creators of knowledge securing and maintaining ③ exclusive access rights to knowledge-based products. For example, in the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industry, where development of new products requires ④ negligible investments with long incubation periods, it is generally acknowledged that companies need exclusive right in order to ⑤ recover research and development costs.
① ownership
② diffusion
③ exclusive
④ negligible
⑤ recover
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ④ (negligible ➔ substantial 또는 massive)
해설: 신약 개발 등의 제약 및 생명공학 산업은 개발 기간이 길고 '상당한/막대한(substantial)' 투자가 요구되는 분야이므로, '하찮은, 무시해도 될 정도의'를 뜻하는 negligible은 문맥상 정반대 의미로 적절하지 않습니다.

[문항 2] 다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오. (빈칸 추론)

In order to have a market in knowledge, intellectual property protection is considered a fundamental instrument for creators of knowledge _________________________________ to knowledge-based products. For example, in the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industry, where development of new products requires substantial investments with long incubation periods, it is generally acknowledged that companies need exclusive right in order to recover research and development costs.
① distributing their innovative findings freely without commercial gain
② securing and maintaining exclusive access rights
③ sharing underlying research data openly with competing enterprises
④ minimizing the institutional barriers imposed on technological diffusion
⑤ transferring proprietary ownership entirely to public governmental sectors
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ②
해설: 지식 시장이 형성되는 핵심 조건은 지식재산권을 통해 창작자가 지식 기반 제품에 대한 '독점적/배타적 접근 권한(exclusive access rights)'을 확보하는 것입니다.

[문항 3] 글의 흐름으로 보아, 주어진 문장이 들어가기에 가장 적절한 곳을 고르시오. (문장 삽입)

[주어진 문장] In order to have a market in knowledge, intellectual property protection is considered a fundamental instrument for creators of knowledge securing and maintaining exclusive access rights to knowledge-based products.

With the rise of the networked communications environment and the continuing progress of scientific advances, knowledge has become an increasingly key factor in fostering innovation. ( ① ) In addition, the ability of people to acquire, access and control knowledge is strictly related to questions pertaining to the ownership of knowledge. ( ② ) At present, intellectual property rules are the principal tool for controlling the creation, diffusion and use of knowledge and related goods and services. ( ③ ) For example, in the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industry, where development of new products requires substantial investments with long incubation periods, companies need exclusive right. ( ④ ) It is generally acknowledged that such legal rights are essential in order to recover research and development costs. ( ⑤ )
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③
해설: 주어진 문장은 지식재산권 보호가 지식 시장의 형성을 가능하게 하는 핵심 도구라는 명제입니다. 지식재산권의 일반적 정의(② 다음)와 제약 산업의 구체적 사례(For example, ③ 다음)를 연결하는 ③번 자리가 가장 적절합니다.

[문항 4] 다음 요약문의 빈칸 (A), (B)에 들어갈 말을 [조건]에 맞게 작성하시오. (서술형)

[요약문] In a knowledge-based economy, granting (A) [ ] rights through intellectual property rules is essential for companies to (B) [ ] their substantial investments in long-term research and development.
[조건]
1. 윗글 본문에 등장하는 단어를 그대로 찾아 쓸 것.
2. (A)는 1단어(형용사), (B)는 1단어(동사원형)로 작성할 것.
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: (A) exclusive / (B) recover
해설: 지식재산권 규범을 통해 '독점적인(exclusive)' 권리를 부여함으로써 막대한 장기 R&D 투자 비용을 '회수하다(recover)'라는 논리적 결론을 완성합니다.
