Measurement as Common Language
Need for Standard Consistency

공동체 확장과 거래 갈등 해소를 위한 측정 단위 일관성의 진화

STEP 01 원문정독 STEP 02 디코딩 STEP 03 논리구조 STEP 04 어법드릴 STEP 05 실전변형
STEP 01 · ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND & FULL PASSAGE

배경지식 & 전체 지문 정독

💡 ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND KNOWLEDGE · 지문 핵심 배경지식
  • [핵심 개념]: 측정의 거리 기술(Technology of Distance)과 표준화된 측정 단위(Standardized Measurement Units)의 일관성
  • [배경 맥락]: 초기 정착 사회의 주관적·신체적 측정 기준은 지역 내에서만 유효했으나, 사회가 팽창하고 무역과 조세 교환이 발생함에 따라 필연적으로 갈등을 유발함
  • [논리 요약]: 측정은 문화와 지리적 격차를 연결하는 일종의 언어이므로, 소통과 무역 분쟁을 해결하기 위해서는 고도의 객관적 일관성과 신뢰할 수 있는 정의가 필수적임
To harness the full potential of units of measurement, we need some degree of consistency. Subjective measures contain useful knowledge, but this can be difficult to share, based as it is on an individual’s experience. The historian of science Theodore M. Porter describes measurement as a ‘technology of distance’: a tool that uses shared rules to bridge differences of culture and geography, allowing for the exchange of information. Following this logic, if measurement is another sort of language, then just as with words, individual units need reliable definitions in order to communicate. In the earliest societies, these definitions might need to be shared no more widely than a single settlement, meaning that the definition can be no more complex than knowing that a unit was equal to a certain body part. As these communities grew in size, though, the inadequacies of this system would become evident. Imagine a disagreement over trade or tribute: it is a moment of conflict that demands greater consistency in measurement.
[핵심 요지] 측정은 지리적·문화적 차이를 넘나드는 공통 언어 역할을 하므로, 공동체가 확장되고 무역과 공납 등의 갈등을 방지하기 위해서는 표준화된 일관성과 신뢰성 있는 정의가 필수적이다.

[변형 1] 다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

To harness the full potential of units of measurement, we need some degree of consistency. The historian of science Theodore M. Porter describes measurement as a ‘technology of distance’: a tool that uses shared rules to bridge differences of culture and geography, allowing for the exchange of information. If measurement is another sort of language, individual units need reliable definitions in order to communicate. In the earliest societies, these definitions could simply be equal to a certain body part within a single settlement. As these communities expanded and engaged in external interactions, however, ________________________ arose, demanding standardized and objective consistency across different regions.
① inevitable moments of conflict over trade and tribute
② voluntary agreements to abandon scientific measurements
③ strict restrictions on sharing technological innovations
④ cultural attempts to preserve subjective body-part standards
⑤ complete collapses of agricultural production systems
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ①
해설: 공동체가 확장됨에 따라 기존의 신체 부위 기반 주관적 단위는 불충분해지고, 무역(trade)이나 공납(tribute)을 둘러싼 의견 불일치와 갈등이 발생하여 측정의 더 큰 일관성을 요구하게 된다는 것이 글의 결론입니다. 따라서 빈칸에는 이러한 '무역과 공납을 둘러싼 필연적인 갈등의 순간들'을 언급한 ①번이 가장 적절합니다.

[주어진 글]
To harness the full potential of units of measurement, we need some degree of consistency. Subjective measures contain useful knowledge, but this can be difficult to share, based as it is on an individual’s experience.

(A) As these communities grew in size, though, the inadequacies of this system would become evident. Imagine a disagreement over trade or tribute: it is a moment of conflict that demands greater consistency in measurement.
(B) The historian of science Theodore M. Porter describes measurement as a ‘technology of distance’: a tool that uses shared rules to bridge differences of culture and geography, allowing for the exchange of information.
(C) Following this logic, if measurement is another sort of language, then individual units need reliable definitions. In the earliest societies, these definitions were simple, meaning that a unit was merely equal to a certain body part.
① (A) - (C) - (B)     ② (B) - (A) - (C)
③ (B) - (C) - (A)     ④ (C) - (A) - (B)
⑤ (C) - (B) - (A)
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③ (B) - (C) - (A)
해설: 주어진 글에서 측정 일관성의 필요성과 주관적 측정의 한계를 제시한 뒤, (B)에서 Porter의 '거리의 기술' 개념을 도입하여 공유 규칙의 필요성을 설명합니다. 이어 (C)에서 'Following this logic'으로 논리를 받아 측정 단위를 언어에 비유하고 초기 소규모 정착지의 단순한 신체 단위 정의를 언급합니다. 마지막으로 (A)에서 'As these communities grew in size, though'로 전환하며 규모 확장에 따른 불충분성과 거래 갈등 해결을 위한 일관성 요구로 매듭짓는 (B) - (C) - (A)가 가장 자연스럽습니다.
