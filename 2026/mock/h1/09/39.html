STEP 01 · ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND & FULL PASSAGE

배경지식 & 전체 지문 정독

💡 ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND KNOWLEDGE · 지문 핵심 배경지식
  • [핵심 개념]: 가위개미(Leaf-cutter Ants)의 균류 농경(Fungus Agriculture) 및 역할 분담(Division of Labor)
  • [배경 맥락]: 가위개미는 식물을 직접 섭취하는 초식 곤충이 아니라 나뭇잎을 지하 정원의 배지(거름)로 활용해 공생 균류를 재배하는 농부임
  • [논리 요약]: 채집개미의 잎 운반과 소형 일개미의 가공·시비, 그리고 균류 버섯체 수확에 이르는 정교한 농경 체계를 통해 거대한 지하 문명을 구축함
With millions of members in a colony, leaf­cutter ants cultivate their own food. Just like humans, they’re farmers. Some of the ants set forth from the nest to find fresh vegetation; when they find it, they chew off large pieces that they carry back to the nest. However, the ants don’t eat these leaves. Instead, smaller worker ants take the pieces of leaves, chew them into smaller pieces, and use these as fertilizer to grow fungus in large underground ‘gardens.’ The ants feed the fungus, and the fungus blossoms into small fruiting bodies which the ants later eat. Using this successful farming strategy, the ants build enormous nests underground, something spanning hundreds of square meters. Just like humans, they have perfected an agricultural civilization.
[핵심 요지] 가위개미는 잎을 직접 먹지 않고 지하 정원에서 균류를 재배하는 비료로 활용하는 고도의 농경 전략과 분업을 통해 거대한 지하 문명을 완성했다.

STEP 05 · EXAM SIMULATION (4 PROBLEMS)

고등부 수능/내신 실전 변형 문제

[변형 1] 다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

With millions of members in a colony, leaf-cutter ants cultivate their own food. Some ants venture out to bring fresh leaves back to the nest. However, the ants do not consume this foliage directly. Instead, smaller worker ants masticate the plant material into finer fragments to use as fertilizer in extensive subterranean chambers. Through this symbiotic cycle, the ants nourish the mold, and the mold generates edible structures. By relying on this cooperative production system, leaf-cutter ants demonstrate that they ________________________, achieving biological feats comparable to human society.
① depend strictly on hunting other competing insect colonies
② have perfected a sophisticated form of agricultural civilization
③ adapt to changing weather by relocating their queen constantly
④ consume toxic vegetation to develop natural internal immunity
⑤ destroy the surrounding ecosystem through aggressive defoliation
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ②
해설: 가위개미는 식물을 직접 먹지 않고 잘게 씹어 거름으로 활용해 균류를 재배하며, 이를 바탕으로 거대한 지하 서식지를 구축합니다. 글의 결론부에 명시된 것처럼 인간과 마찬가지로 정교한 농경 문명을 완성했다(have perfected an agricultural civilization)는 내용의 ②번이 가장 적절합니다.

[변형 2] 주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

[주어진 글]
With millions of members in a colony, leaf­cutter ants cultivate their own food. Just like humans, they’re farmers. Some of the ants set forth from the nest to find fresh vegetation.

(A) The ants feed the fungus, and the fungus blossoms into small fruiting bodies which the ants later eat. Using this farming strategy, they build enormous nests underground, spanning hundreds of square meters.
(B) When they find it, they chew off large pieces that they carry back to the nest. However, the ants don’t eat these leaves.
(C) Instead, smaller worker ants take the pieces of leaves, chew them into smaller pieces, and use these as fertilizer to grow fungus in large underground ‘gardens.’ Just like humans, they have perfected an agricultural civilization.
① (A) - (C) - (B)     ② (B) - (A) - (C)
③ (B) - (C) - (A)     ④ (C) - (A) - (B)
⑤ (C) - (B) - (A)
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③ (B) - (C) - (A)
해설: 주어진 글에서 신선한 초목을 찾으러 나선다는 내용에 이어, (B)에서 초목을 발견하여 잎을 잘라 운반하지만 잎을 먹지 않는다는 반전(However, the ants don’t eat these leaves)이 옵니다. 그 뒤 (C)에서 Instead(그 대신에)로 연결되며 소형 일개미들이 잎을 씹어 균류의 비료로 쓴다는 설명이 나오고, (A)에서 균류가 자실체를 맺어 개미가 이를 먹고 거대한 서식지를 구축해 문명을 완성한다는 흐름으로 완결되는 (B) - (C) - (A)가 가장 자연스럽습니다.

[변형 3] 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?

With millions of members in a colony, leaf-cutter ants cultivate their own food. Some of the ants set forth from the nest to find fresh vegetation; when they find it, they chew off large pieces that they carry back to the nest. However, the ants ① refuse to eat these leaves directly. Instead, smaller worker ants chew them into smaller pieces and use these as ② fertilizer to grow fungus in underground chambers. The ants feed the fungus, and the fungus develops into fruiting bodies which the ants later consume. Using this ③ inefficient farming strategy, the ants build ④ enormous nests underground, demonstrating that they have perfected an agricultural system.
① refuse     ② fertilizer     ③ inefficient     ④ enormous
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③ inefficient → successful
해설: 가위개미는 이러한 농경 전략을 통해 수백 제곱미터에 달하는 거대한 지하 서식지를 구축하고 수백만 마리의 군집을 유지하므로, 농경 전략은 '비효율적인(inefficient)' 것이 아니라 '성공적인(successful)' 것이어야 문맥상 올바릅니다.
