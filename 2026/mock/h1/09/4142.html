Internet Democratizes Slang
Dismantling Linguistic Barriers

인터넷과 소셜 미디어가 이끈 규범 파괴와 속어의 대중화

STEP 01 · ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND & FULL PASSAGE

배경지식 & 전체 지문 정독

💡 ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND KNOWLEDGE · 지문 핵심 배경지식
  • [핵심 개념]: 언어적 규범주의(Prescriptive Norms)의 붕괴 및 소셜 미디어를 통한 속어의 민주화(Democratization of Slang)
  • [배경 맥락]: 과거 인쇄·방송 미디어 체제에서는 표준어 중심의 언어 규칙 제정자(rule makers)가 소통 통로를 독점 통제했음
  • [논리 요약]: 소셜 미디어 플랫폼과 크리에이터들의 부상은 기존 언어 장벽을 허물고 속어에 정당성을 부여하여 급속한 밈 확산을 촉진함
The role of ‘slang’ in popular culture has fundamentally changed because of the internet. Since the emergence of Standard English, the rule makers of language have been in charge, and their prescriptive norms about a ‘correct’ version of the English language have dominated our channels of communication. If you wanted your voice to be heard by the public, you had to work within their limits, conforming to the strict expectations of either print or broadcast media. Social media completely broke that barrier. The playing field is now level, and public access is democratized. Anybody and everybody can become a public figure on the new communicative platforms, meaning that nobody’s left to enforce the old rules. TV presenters and journalists might not have been able to use slang, but content creators can. We regularly use words like ‘function’ and ‘side­eye,’ and our influence both legitimizes these terms and gives them a large platform to spread quicker than they could have before. Content creators also come from a greater variety of backgrounds than people working in traditional media. This means that slang can more naturally go beyond the economic and social barriers that once limited its growth. The loose social networks that previously connected us geographically are simply stronger online, and memes are now transmitted at rapid pace.
[핵심 요지] 인터넷과 소셜 미디어는 기존 전통 매체의 표준어 통제 규범을 해체하고, 다양한 배경의 크리에이터들을 통해 속어를 공론장의 정당한 언어로 확산시키며 언어의 민주화를 이끌었다.

STEP 05 · EXAM SIMULATION (4 PROBLEMS)

고등부 수능/내신 실전 변형 문제

[변형 1] 다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

Historically, the rule makers of language controlled traditional print and broadcast channels, forcing public speakers to conform strictly to prescriptive linguistic norms. However, the rise of social media dismantled these centralized filters. Because communicative platforms allow anyone to become a public figure without institutional gatekeepers, ________________________. As diverse content creators gain massive followings, unconventional terms and slang that once struggled against socioeconomic boundaries now acquire instant legitimacy and circulate across digital networks at an unprecedented pace.
① professional journalists maintain absolute authority over grammar
② the traditional gatekeeping mechanisms enforcing linguistic rules have dissolved
③ local regional dialects have been completely eradicated by global standards
④ internet users are legally penalized for utilizing nonstandard vocabulary
⑤ commercial advertisers dictate which slang terms qualify as legitimate language
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ②
해설: 소셜 미디어가 소통 장벽을 허물고 누구나 발언권을 얻게 되면서 과거의 낡은 언어 규칙을 강제할 수 있는 전통적 문지기(gatekeepers)와 통제 체계가 사라졌다는 내용(meaning that nobody’s left to enforce the old rules)이 들어가야 하므로 ②번이 가장 적절합니다.

[변형 2] 주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

[주어진 글]
The role of ‘slang’ in popular culture has fundamentally changed because of the internet. Since the emergence of Standard English, the rule makers of language have dominated our channels of communication.

(A) Content creators also come from a greater variety of backgrounds than people working in traditional media. This means that slang can more naturally go beyond the economic and social barriers that once limited its growth.
(B) If you wanted your voice to be heard by the public, you had to conform to print or broadcast expectations. Social media completely broke that barrier, democratizing public access and leaving nobody to enforce the old rules.
(C) TV presenters might not have been able to use slang, but modern content creators can. Their regular usage legitimizes these terms and provides a large platform to spread them quicker than ever before.
① (A) - (C) - (B)     ② (B) - (A) - (C)
③ (B) - (C) - (A)     ④ (C) - (A) - (B)
⑤ (C) - (B) - (A)
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③ (B) - (C) - (A)
해설: 주어진 글의 전통적 언어 규칙 통제에 이어, (B)에서 대중 매체의 한계와 소셜 미디어가 장벽을 허문 변화를 설명합니다. 이어 (C)에서 과거 방송인과 달리 크리에이터들이 속어를 자유롭게 사용하며 정당성을 부여하는 현상을 서술하고, (A)에서 'Content creators also come from...'로 이어지며 크리에이터들의 다양한 사회적 배경 덕분에 속어가 기존 장벽을 넘어 급속 전파된다는 결론으로 귀결되는 (B) - (C) - (A) 순서가 가장 자연스럽습니다.
