STEP 01 · ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND & FULL PASSAGE

배경지식 & 전체 지문 정독

💡 ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND KNOWLEDGE · 지문 핵심 배경지식
  • [핵심 개념]: 자존감의 사회측정기 이론(Sociometer Theory of Self-Esteem)과 진화심리학적 관계 가치(Relational Value)
  • [배경 맥락]: 원시 수렵채집 사회에서 집단 배척은 곧 죽음을 의미했으므로, 인간은 자신의 포섭 상태(inclusion status)를 실시간 감지하는 내적 모니터링 경보기를 진화시킴
  • [논리 요약]: 자존감은 집단 내 수용 여부와 관계적 가치를 무의식적으로 감시하다가 거절 위험이 감지되면 의식적 경보를 울려 행동 조정을 유도하는 생존 기제임
From an evolutionary perspective, self­esteem has developed as a monitoring mechanism for the quality of our relationships. The system monitors other people’s reactions to our behaviors and sends an alarm that alerts us to any possible changes in our inclusion status. The monitoring system generally operates nonconsciously until it detects that our relational value is falling or low. When the alarm bell rings, the threat to our social bonds is pushed into conscious awareness and we evaluate the situation. Am I being accepted or am I being pushed out? Do I need to adjust my behaviors to fix the relationship? With such a high value for survival closely tied with relationships, it makes sense that the way we mobilize our internal resources around relationships would be highly networked in our brain and body. It also makes sense that we would develop specific mechanisms to attend to minimizing rejection and enhancing acceptance. The continuous scanning is normal, a part of being human. We all have an interpersonal process of searching for cues from the environment to assess our value in the social structure.
[핵심 요지] 자존감은 집단에서의 배척을 최소화하고 수용을 극대화하기 위해 관계적 가치와 포섭 상태를 끊임없이 탐지하고 경보를 울리는 진화론적 생존 모니터링 체계이다.

고등부 수능/내신 실전 변형 문제

[변형 1] 다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

From an evolutionary perspective, self­esteem has developed as a monitoring mechanism for the quality of our relationships. The system tracks other people’s reactions to our behaviors and sends an alarm that alerts us to any possible changes in our inclusion status. This psychological apparatus operates nonconsciously until it detects a decline in our social standing. When the alarm bell rings, the threat to our interpersonal bonds enters conscious awareness, compelling us to evaluate whether we are accepted or pushed out. Because ancestral human survival was inextricably linked to group membership, our brain and body have evolved to ________________________ to safeguard our social value.
① suppress individual emotional reactions to preserve total independence
② prioritize short-term physical pleasures over long-term communal stability
③ continuously scan the environment for cues that minimize social rejection
④ disregard negative social signals to maintain artificially inflated confidence
⑤ eliminate competitive instincts entirely to promote unconditional altruism
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③
해설: 지문의 후반부에서 인간은 진화론적으로 생존을 위해 거절을 최소화하고(minimizing rejection) 수용을 높이기 위한 특정 메커니즘을 발전시켰으며, 환경으로부터 단서를 지속적으로 탐색(continuous scanning)한다고 설명하고 있습니다. 따라서 빈칸에는 사회적 거절을 최소화하기 위해 환경의 단서들을 상시 탐색한다는 내용의 ③번이 가장 적절합니다.

[변형 2] 주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

[주어진 글]
From an evolutionary perspective, self­esteem has developed as a monitoring mechanism for the quality of our relationships. The system monitors other people’s reactions to our behaviors and sends an alarm.

(A) With such a high value for survival closely tied with relationships, it makes sense that the way we mobilize our internal resources would be highly networked in our brain and body to minimize rejection and enhance acceptance.
(B) The monitoring system generally operates nonconsciously until it detects that our relational value is falling or low. When the alarm bell rings, the threat to our social bonds is pushed into conscious awareness.
(C) In this conscious state, we evaluate the situation by asking whether we are being accepted or pushed out, and whether we need to adjust our behaviors to fix the relationship.
① (A) - (C) - (B)     ② (B) - (A) - (C)
③ (B) - (C) - (A)     ④ (C) - (A) - (B)
⑤ (C) - (B) - (A)
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③ (B) - (C) - (A)
해설: 주어진 글에서 자존감이라는 감시 경보 체계를 소개한 뒤, (B)에서 평소에는 무의식적으로 작동하다가 관계적 가치가 떨어질 때 경보가 울려 의식적 자각으로 전환되는 과정을 설명합니다. 이어서 (C)에서 'In this conscious state'로 받아 수용 여부를 평가하고 행동을 조정하려는 질문들을 구체화하고, 마지막으로 (A)에서 생존과 직결된 관계를 지키기 위해 뇌와 신체가 거절을 줄이고 수용을 늘리도록 네트워크화되었다는 진화적 결론으로 귀결되는 (B) - (C) - (A) 순서가 가장 자연스럽습니다.

[변형 3] 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?

From an evolutionary perspective, self­esteem has developed as a monitoring mechanism for the quality of our relationships. The system monitors other people’s reactions and alerts us to changes in our inclusion status. This system operates ① nonconsciously until it detects that our relational value is declining. When the alarm rings, the threat to our social bonds enters conscious awareness, prompting us to ② evaluate whether we need to adjust our behaviors. Because human survival is deeply tied to social connection, it makes sense that we have developed mechanisms aimed at ③ maximizing rejection and enhancing acceptance. ① nonconsciously     ② evaluate     ③ maximizing     ④ value
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③ maximizing → minimizing
해설: 원시 사회에서 타인으로부터의 거절(rejection)은 생존에 치명적인 위협이었으므로, 인간이 진화시킨 심리적 기제의 목표는 거절을 '극대화(maximizing)'하는 것이 아니라 '최소화(minimizing)'하고 집단의 수용(acceptance)을 증진시키는 것입니다. 따라서 ③의 maximizing은 글의 전체 논지와 반대되므로 어색합니다.
