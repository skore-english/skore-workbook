Social Nature of Taste
Food Identity and Conformity

음식 취향은 고유한 특성보다 사회적 관계와 정체성에 의해 결정된다

STEP 01 원문정독 STEP 02 디코딩 STEP 03 논리구조 STEP 04 어법드릴 STEP 05 실전변형
STEP 01 · ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND & FULL PASSAGE

배경지식 & 전체 지문 정독

💡 ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND KNOWLEDGE · 지문 핵심 배경지식
  • [핵심 개념]: 취향의 사회적 구성(Social Construction of Taste) 및 사회적 구별짓기(Social Differentiation)
  • [배경 맥락]: 음식 취향은 음식 자체의 물리적 맛이나 질보다 주변 인간관계, 소속 집단, 그리고 '내가 누구인가'를 규정하는 정체성 욕구에 의해 전파됨
  • [논리 요약]: 개인의 입맛은 유아기부터 주변인들과의 상호작용 속에서 바이러스처럼 확산되며, 타인의 소비 방식을 비판적으로 관찰하고 동조하거나 회피하면서 형성됨
Our tastes are social. Even when you first drank milk, there was the milk, yes, but there was also the person behind the milk who will have presumably either made or messed up your relationship with food for the couple of decades after that. We learn to eat from our families and friends, at school and in work. A village develops a particular recipe for an apple pie ― one that nobody outside the village likes, but who cares? We want these things not just because of what they are, but who we are. Tastes spread like this, person to person, like viruses, almost irrespective of the qualities of the food involved. We observe other people in their food lives, often judgmentally. Nothing turns me off something quicker than the wrong people liking it, by which I mean people almost identical to me in every respect, in a way that doesn’t flatter me. I avoided the viral chopped salad moment of 2024 by the skin of my teeth.
[핵심 요지] 우리의 음식 취향은 음식 자체의 객관적 속성이 아니라, 타인과의 사회적 관계, 집단적 정체성, 그리고 타인을 향한 평가적 태도에 의해 형성되고 바이러스처럼 전파된다.

고등부 수능/내신 실전 변형 문제

[변형 1] 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?

Our tastes are social. Even when you first drank milk, there was also the person behind the milk who shaped your relationship with food. We learn to eat from our families and friends. A village develops a particular recipe for an apple pie ― one that nobody outside the village likes, but who cares? We want these things not just because of what they are, but who we are. Tastes spread like this, person to person, like viruses, almost ① irrespective of the qualities of the food involved. We observe other people in their food lives, often ② judgmentally. Nothing turns me off something quicker than the ③ wrong people liking it, by which I mean people almost identical to me in a way that doesn’t flatter me. In this sense, individual taste is largely an outcome of social ④ isolation, as our culinary preferences continuously reflect how we want to be seen by others.
① irrespective     ② judgmentally     ③ wrong     ④ isolation
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ④ isolation → interaction / conformity / connection
해설: 글의 핵심 논지는 개인의 음식 취향이 홀로 고립되어 형성되는 것이 아니라, 가족, 친구, 마을 사람들 등 타인과의 사회적 관계 및 상호작용(social interaction)을 통해 바이러스처럼 퍼진다는 것입니다. 따라서 ④의 isolation(고립)은 글의 주제와 정면으로 배치되므로 적절하지 않습니다.

[주어진 글]
Our tastes are social. Even when you first drank milk, there was the milk, yes, but there was also the person behind the milk who will have presumably either made or messed up your relationship with food for decades.

(A) Tastes spread like this, person to person, like viruses, almost irrespective of the qualities of the food involved. We observe other people in their food lives, often judgmentally.
(B) Nothing turns me off something quicker than the wrong people liking it, by which I mean people almost identical to me in a way that doesn’t flatter me. I avoided the viral chopped salad moment of 2024 by the skin of my teeth.
(C) We learn to eat from our families and friends, at school and in work. A village develops a particular recipe for an apple pie ― one that nobody outside the village likes. We want these things not just because of what they are, but who we are.
① (A) - (C) - (B)     ② (B) - (A) - (C)
③ (B) - (C) - (A)     ④ (C) - (A) - (B)
⑤ (C) - (B) - (A)
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ④ (C) - (A) - (B)
해설: 주어진 글에서 우유를 먹여준 양육자와의 관계를 제시한 후, (C)에서 가족, 친구, 마을의 애플파이 사례를 들며 '우리가 누구인가(who we are)'라는 사회적 정체성 학습으로 논의를 확장합니다. 이어 (A)에서 'Tastes spread like this(취향은 이처럼 퍼진다)'로 받으며 음식의 질과 무관한 바이러스 같은 확산과 타인에 대한 평가적 관찰을 언급합니다. 마지막으로 (B)에서 타인의 취향에 반발하여 유행을 거부하는 개인적 일화로 매듭짓는 (C) - (A) - (B) 전개가 가장 자연스럽습니다.

[변형 3] 글의 흐름으로 보아, 주어진 문장이 들어가기에 가장 적절한 곳을 고르시오.

[주어진 문장]
We want these things not just because of what they are, but who we are.

Our tastes are social. Even when you first drank milk, there was the milk, yes, but there was also the person behind the milk who shaped your food habits. ( ① ) We learn to eat from our families and friends, at school and in work. ( ② ) A village develops a particular recipe for an apple pie ― one that nobody outside the village likes, but who cares? ( ③ ) Tastes spread like this, person to person, like viruses, almost irrespective of the qualities of the food involved. ( ④ ) We observe other people in their food lives, often judgmentally. ( ⑤ ) Nothing turns me off something quicker than the wrong people liking it, by which I mean people almost identical to me in every respect.
①       ②       ③       ④       ⑤
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③
해설: 주어진 문장의 'these things'는 바로 앞 문장(② 뒤)에 나온 '마을 외부 사람들은 좋아하지 않는 그 마을만의 사과파이'를 가리킵니다. 마을 사람들이 그 파이를 원하는 이유는 음식 자체의 맛(what they are) 때문이 아니라 자신들의 정체성(who we are) 때문이라는 설명이 이어진 후, ③ 뒤의 '취향은 이처럼(like this) 음식의 질과 상관없이 퍼져 나간다'는 문장으로 긴밀하게 연결됩니다.
