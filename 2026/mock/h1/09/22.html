STEP 01 · ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND & FULL PASSAGE

배경지식 & 전체 지문 정독

💡 ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND KNOWLEDGE · 지문 핵심 배경지식
  • [핵심 개념]: 끌어당김의 법칙(Law of Attraction)에 대한 통념적 오류와 내재적 신념 체계(Belief System)의 결정적 역할
  • [배경 맥락]: 단순한 시각화(Visualization)나 긍정적 확언(Affirmation)만으로는 자신의 무의식적 자기 부정 및 제한적 신념을 이길 수 없음
  • [논리 요약]: 진정한 실현(Manifestation)을 위해서는 표면적 상상에 그치지 않고 자신의 욕망과 상충하는 내면의 제한적 신념을 찾아 개선해야 함
Perhaps you have heard of the Law of Attraction, which states that ‘like attracts like’ and that by focusing on positive or negative thoughts, one can bring about positive or negative results. The common flaw in our understanding of this law is that we believe all we have to do is think about or visualize something to manifest it. It is true that we attract at the level of our own vibration and that our thoughts and words are extremely magnetic. But the most powerful attractor is our belief system. You can create beautiful poster boards full of images of what you want and affirm every day that you are available for an incredibly successful, passionate career or a loving relationship; but if you don’t truly believe you are enough, worthy, lovable, and deserving, then attracting a great career or relationship into your life may be more challenging. Consider what limiting beliefs you have that contradict your desires and upgrade them to beliefs that are in line with what you want to attract.
[핵심 요지] 긍정적 생각이나 시각화만으로는 부족하며, 원하는 결과를 끌어당기기 위해서는 내면의 잠재적 제한 신념을 찾아 욕망과 일치하도록 바꿔야 한다.

Perhaps you have heard of the Law of Attraction, which states that ‘like attracts like’ and that by focusing on positive or negative thoughts, one can bring about positive or negative results. The common flaw in our understanding of this law is that we believe all we have to do is think about or visualize something to manifest it. ④ ambitious career goals inevitably lead to frustration

정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ②
해설: 생각이나 시각화만으로 끌어당김이 일어난다는 통념을 뒤집으며, 아무리 상상하고 확언해도 스스로 자격이 있다고 믿지 않으면 실현되기 어렵다고 설명하고 있습니다. 따라서 끌어당김에서 가장 핵심적이고 결정적인 역할을 하는 것은 '우리의 신념 체계(our belief system)'라는 내용의 ②번이 가장 적절합니다.

[변형 2] 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?

Perhaps you have heard of the Law of Attraction. The common ① flaw in our understanding of this law is that we believe all we have to do is think about or visualize something to manifest it. It is true that our thoughts and words are extremely magnetic. But the most powerful attractor is our belief system. ① flaw     ② available     ③ deserving     ④ support    
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ④ support → contradict
해설: 문맥상 자신이 원하는 바(욕망)를 가로막는 '제한적 신념(limiting beliefs)'을 찾아내어 원하는 것과 일치하도록 개선해야 한다는 흐름입니다. 따라서 자신의 욕망을 지지(support)하는 것이 아니라, 욕망과 모순되고 상충하는(contradict) 신념을 파악해야 하므로 ④의 support는 어색합니다.

[변형 3] 글의 흐름으로 보아, 주어진 문장이 들어가기에 가장 적절한 곳을 고르시오.

[주어진 문장]
But the most powerful attractor is our belief system.

Perhaps you have heard of the Law of Attraction, which states that ‘like attracts like’ and that by focusing on positive or negative thoughts, one can bring about positive or negative results. ( ① ) The common flaw in our understanding of this law is that we believe all we have to do is think about or visualize something to manifest it. ( ② ) It is true that we attract at the level of our own vibration and that our thoughts and words are extremely magnetic. ( ③ ) You can create beautiful poster boards full of images of what you want and affirm every day that you are available for an incredibly successful, passionate career or a loving relationship. ( ④ ) But if you don’t truly believe you are enough, worthy, lovable, and deserving, then attracting a great career or relationship into your life may be more challenging. ( ⑤ ) Consider what limiting beliefs you have that contradict your desires and upgrade them to beliefs that are in line with what you want to attract.
①       ②       ③       ④       ⑤
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③
해설: 주어진 문장은 생각이나 말보다 더 본질적인 것으로 '신념 체계'를 제시하는 전환 문장입니다. ③ 앞에서는 생각과 말이 자성을 띤다는 진술(양보적 인정)이 나오고, ③ 뒤에서는 예시로서 아무리 이미지 보드를 만들고 확언해도 '자신이 자격이 있다는 신념'이 없으면 실패한다는 구체적 설명이 이어지므로 ③의 자리가 가장 자연스럽습니다.
