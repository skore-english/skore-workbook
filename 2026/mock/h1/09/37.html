Copernicus to Galileo
Evolution of Cosmic Understanding

STEP 01 · ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND & FULL PASSAGE

배경지식 & 전체 지문 정독

💡 ESSENTIAL BACKGROUND KNOWLEDGE · 지문 핵심 배경지식
  • [핵심 개념]: 천동설(Geocentric Model)에서 지동설(Heliocentric Theory)로의 코페르니쿠스적 전환(Copernican Revolution)과 관측 기술의 진보
  • [배경 맥락]: 고대 인류의 육안 천체 관측부터 16세기 코페르니쿠스의 혁신적 가설 제시, 그리고 17세기 갈릴레오의 망원경을 통한 실증적 검증 과정
  • [논리 요약]: 코페르니쿠스는 육안 관측으로 태양 중심설을 주장했고, 이후 갈릴레이가 광학 망원경 관측을 통해 이를 실증하며 우주에 대한 인류의 지식을 획기적으로 진보시킴
Even before telescopes existed, people looked out into space and tried to understand the universe. The Egyptians, Babylonians, Greeks, and Maya were among the first peoples to map the movements of the stars. In Europe, a 16th­century Polish mathematician named Nicolaus Copernicus put forth a revolutionary idea. Copernicus watched the stars and took measurements by eye. He concluded that the view of the universe accepted at the time ─ that the Sun traveled around Earth ─ was wrong, and that it was in fact Earth that traveled around the Sun. Despite widespread objections to his ideas, he published his theory in a book. Later astronomers built on Copernicus’ work. Technological improvements led to the development of the telescope in the early 17th century. Using a telescope, the Italian scientist Galileo Galilei discovered the four largest moons of Jupiter and observed craters on Earth’s Moon. He also confirmed that Copernicus’ theory was correct. Over the centuries, scientific discoveries and information from telescopes advanced our knowledge of how the universe works.
[핵심 요지] 코페르니쿠스는 육안 관측을 통해 천동설의 오류를 밝히고 지동설을 제안하였으며, 이후 갈릴레이가 망원경 관측을 통해 이를 실증함으로써 인류의 우주에 대한 과학적 이해가 진보했다.

STEP 05 · EXAM SIMULATION (4 PROBLEMS)

고등부 수능/내신 실전 변형 문제

[변형 1] 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?

Even before telescopes existed, ancient civilizations tried to map the movements of the stars. In Europe, Nicolaus Copernicus put forth a revolutionary idea. Copernicus watched the stars and took measurements by eye. He concluded that the view of the universe accepted at the time ─ that the Sun traveled around Earth ─ was ① wrong, and that Earth traveled around the Sun. Despite widespread ② approvals to his ideas, he published his theory in a book. Later astronomers built on his work. In the early 17th century, using a newly developed telescope, Galileo Galilei discovered Jupiter’s moons and observed lunar craters. He also ③ confirmed that Copernicus’ theory was ④ correct, thereby advancing our understanding of how the universe operates.
① wrong     ② approvals     ③ confirmed     ④ correct
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ② approvals → objections
해설: 당시에는 지구가 중심이라는 천동설이 지배적이었으므로, 지구가 태양 주위를 돈다는 코페르니쿠스의 혁명적 이론에 대해 사회적 찬성이나 승인(approvals)이 쏟아진 것이 아니라 '광범위한 반대(widespread objections)'가 일어났습니다. 따라서 ②의 approvals는 문맥상 정반대 단어입니다.

[변형 2] 다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

Even before telescopes existed, ancient peoples mapped the movements of the stars by eye. In the 16th century, Copernicus challenged the prevailing geocentric model and argued that Earth revolves around the Sun. Later, the invention of the telescope in the early 17th century marked a critical turning point. Galileo Galilei used this new optical device to discover the four largest moons of Jupiter and observe craters on the Moon. These empirical observations were profoundly significant because they ________________________, transforming human understanding of the cosmos from pure mathematical speculation into evidence-based science.
① proved that the ancient geocentric worldview was indisputably accurate
② established that planetary movements are entirely chaotic and unpredictable
③ confirmed the correctness of Copernicus' heliocentric theory through direct evidence
④ discouraged astronomers from relying on technological tools for cosmic research
⑤ demonstrated that Earth is the stationary center around which all celestial bodies orbit
정답 및 해설 확인
정답: ③
해설: 빈칸 앞뒤 문맥은 갈릴레이가 망원경을 사용해 목성의 위성과 달의 분화구를 관찰한 경험적 증거의 의의를 묻고 있습니다. 본문 후반부에 명시된 것처럼 갈릴레이의 관측은 코페르니쿠스의 이론이 옳았음을 확증(confirmed that Copernicus' theory was correct)했습니다. 따라서 이를 반영한 ③번이 가장 적절합니다.
